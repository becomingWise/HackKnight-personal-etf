How does S&P 500 weighting work?

A company's weight is its share price × publicly tradable shares, divided by the total float-adjusted market value of all companies in the index.

Here the denominator is only your selected stocks. Equal weighting gives each selection the same share. Event scenarios never change these allocations.

To enable market-cap weighting, supply sourced USD float-adjusted values from the same date (within 30 days). Download the import format. Blank example values must be replaced.

THE SMALL PRINT THAT MATTERS

A relationship is a clue.
It isn't a promise.

Event links come from the team's business-exposure map. Statistical strength comes from this stock's actual historical prices and the same market's odds history. The scenario estimates a same-day stock return relative to SPY, holding the broader market's return fixed. It does not estimate a future price.

Odds are 12-hour snapshots, sampled before each US close. We show overlapping days, an interval for the fitted relationship, and whether its direction held in a later sample.
